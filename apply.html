Application Requirements & Deadlines

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or passport copy
  • Recent photograph of student
  • Academic records from previous 2 years
  • Teacher recommendations (2 required)
  • Standardized test scores (if available)

Application Deadlines

  • Priority Deadline: January 15, 2024
  • Regular Deadline: March 1, 2024
  • Rolling Admissions: After March 1 (space available)
  • Financial Aid Deadline: February 1, 2024
  • Decision Notification: By April 1, 2024

Assessment Process

  • Grades K-2: Play-based assessment
  • Grades 3-5: Academic screening
  • Grades 6-8: Entrance exam & interview
  • Grades 9-12: Transcript review & interview
  • All grades: Parent interview

Application FAQs

Common questions about the application process

The application fee is $75 and is non-refundable. This fee covers the cost of processing your application, including document review and assessment administration. Fee waivers are available for families with demonstrated financial need.

Yes, you can apply for multiple children using the same parent account. Each child will need a separate application, but you can use the same login to manage all applications. Sibling applications are reviewed together when possible.

Admission decisions are based on a holistic review of each applicant. We consider academic records, teacher recommendations, assessment results, extracurricular involvement, and alignment with our school's mission and values. We also consider the applicant's potential to contribute to and benefit from our educational community.

We accept applications on a rolling basis after the regular deadline, subject to space availability. Late applications are considered as spaces become available. However, financial aid may be limited for late applicants, so we encourage submitting by the priority deadline if possible.

For applications submitted by the priority deadline (January 15), decisions are typically released by March 15. For regular deadline applications (March 1), decisions are released by April 1. Rolling admission decisions are typically made within 2-3 weeks of completing the application process.
