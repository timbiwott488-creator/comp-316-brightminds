Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

School hours vary by division. Elementary school runs from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, middle school from 8:00 AM to 3:20 PM, and high school from 7:45 AM to 3:30 PM. Early dismissal days end at 12:00 PM for elementary, 12:30 PM for middle school, and 12:45 PM for high school.

You can schedule a campus tour by contacting our admissions office at (555) 123-4568 or admissions@brightminds.edu. Tours are typically available Monday through Friday at 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM. We also offer special open house events throughout the year.

For the 2024-2025 school year, the priority application deadline is January 15, 2024. Applications received after this date will be considered on a space-available basis. We encourage early application as some grades fill quickly.

Yes, Bright Minds Academy offers need-based financial aid to qualified families. Approximately 30% of our students receive some form of financial assistance. The financial aid application deadline is February 1, 2024. Contact our admissions office for more information.

We follow all local health department guidelines. Current measures include enhanced cleaning protocols, improved ventilation systems, and availability of hand sanitizer throughout campus. We monitor local conditions and adjust protocols as needed to ensure student and staff safety.
